What to judge

  • Does the primary action dominate? In A it sits below five rows. If your eye does not land on it first, the hierarchy has failed.
  • Is the prescription legible at a glance? Name and sets should resolve without reading the whole screen.
  • Is “optional” quiet enough to skip past, but present enough to find?
  • Does B read as composed or as unfinished? One optional hold, no scrolling.
  • Ink or Clay for the primary button? Ink keeps the accent rare; Clay is warmer but puts a lot of terracotta on screen.
  • Is the serif worth its download? It carries exactly one line per screen.
  • Does the paper grain hold up on a real Samsung at low brightness?
